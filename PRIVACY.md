# Viewfinder 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 26 日

## 中文

Viewfinder 不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人数据。

- **相机**：只用来在屏幕上显示取景画面和拍照。画面在手机本地处理，不会离开你的设备。
- **相册**：只用来保存你拍下的照片。App 只申请“添加照片”的权限，无法读取你相册里已有的照片。
- **设置**：你的偏好设置（如对焦屏、语言）只保存在本机。
- App 不包含任何广告、统计分析或第三方 SDK，也不需要联网。

如有疑问，请联系：marskwan8@gmail.com

## English

Viewfinder does not collect, store or upload any personal data.

- **Camera**: used only to show the viewfinder image and take photos. Images are processed on your device and never leave it.
- **Photos**: used only to save the photos you take. The app asks for add-only access and cannot read the photos already in your library.
- **Settings**: your preferences (such as the focusing screen and language) are stored only on your device.
- The app contains no ads, analytics or third-party SDKs, and needs no network connection.
